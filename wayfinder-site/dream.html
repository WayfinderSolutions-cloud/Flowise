Hunters of the Dagromirren

On the first day of every year from the first year after birth, the Dagromirren young are taken on a hunt by their guardian. The first years are spent hunting small creatures, crafty bugs or small rodents. The next years are taken to hunting quick or swift prey. Then after a decade the children begin to hunt dangerous creatures with their fathers and mothers.

On a young dagromir's sixteenth birthday, they are released at dawn. They have full access to the armory, the stables, the craftsmen, the blacksmiths. The entire community is present and at service to the coming of age of the young hunter. Not one member of the community is allowed to speak to the young hunter until they return with the harvest of a beast.

Primary Witnesses

ALETH Dagromirren East Reach

"It's really not personal." Aleth said to the wounded kanis. "I take your bounty, I return accepted."

The kanis growled, baring it's fangs, limping from the entry point of the arrow Aleth had shot. Aleth glanced back to where their horse, where the flutter of wings could be heard. Turning back again to the undergrowth where the large animal had almost been cornered, just in time to see it look up to the sky and then bolt off in the oposite direction. Aleth quickly raised their bow and drew back an arrow, following the kanis.

DARKNESS

The day shifted to night over Aleth in a split second, the darkness slowly creeped to where the kanis had been. Aleth blinked once, twice, then looked up. There was no sky, only pitch black, somehow writhing, moving, contracting, then WHOOOSH eardrums popped as Aleth was knocked to the floor by some unseen pressure. A moment of dazed confusion passed, and then upon opening eyes, the dream was over. Daylight had returned. The kanis had escaped. Aleth still had no harvest. Aleth tried to have no fear.

LIYAN Dagromirren East Reach

Liyan sat at a familiar perch on the watchtower looking out towards the east. It had been four days. Four days since they had gone for the hunt. Liyan cursed their own upcoming hunt, wishing it would come sooner so they could be reunited, in life or in death.

Each hunter was given seven days to fulfill their mission before the hunters would gather and search for them. Liyan prepared to hunt tomorrow. The day of their own hunt was upon them. The hunt meant little with Aleth still not returned.

Liyan knew where Aleth would have gone, knew how to follow a trail. They had rarely been separated since childhood, this perhaps being the longest they had gone without seeing each other. Liyan did not grieve for Aleth, who was their own strength. Liyan ached from the fear of loss.

"Tomorrow," thought Liyan. "Tomorrow I will find you."
